What We Haul Away

If it's heavy, bulky, or just plain in your way — The Dumping Dudes will clear it out. Below is a rundown of our most requested services.

Residential Junk Removal

Reclaim your space room by room. We clear garages, basements, attics, and everything in between.

  • Garage & basement clutter
  • Broken appliances
  • General household junk

Commercial Junk Removal

Office moves, retail resets, and warehouse cleanouts handled quickly with minimal disruption to your day.

  • Office furniture & equipment
  • Retail fixtures & displays

Keep your job site clear and your project on schedule. We haul it out so your crew can keep working.

  • General contractor cleanup

Heavy items hauled out safely — no scratched floors, dinged walls, or straining your back.

  • Sofas, mattresses & bed frames
  • Treadmills & heavy gym equipment

Yard Waste & Storm Debris

Clear the yard after a big cleanup, storm, or landscaping project so you can enjoy the space again.

  • Storm & fallen tree debris

Estate & Hoarding Cleanouts

A sensitive job deserves a patient, judgment-free crew. We work at your pace and treat every item with respect.
